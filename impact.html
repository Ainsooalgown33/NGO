Case Study: Akufo Outreach

During our major outreach in Akufo, Ibadan, our team successfully deployed a dual-action intervention. We managed crowd-control logistics to serve over 100 vulnerable community members, running a simultaneous Medical Tent (distributing free drugs and conducting malaria/BP checks) and our signature ₦100 Subsidized Food Market. It was a testament to what we can achieve when partners and volunteers unite.
