Our Interventions

We tackle extreme poverty and poor health access through three main pillars of action.

🛒 The ₦100 Market & Welfare

To soothe the struggle caused by economic hardship, we establish unique trade fair-style markets in rural areas. Essential food items (rice, beans, yam) and household goods are sold at a highly subsidized flat rate of just ₦100, restoring dignity while providing relief.

🩺 Mobile Medical Tents

We deploy volunteer healthcare professionals (doctors, nurses, pharmacists) to marginalized communities to provide free medical check-ups, blood pressure monitoring, malaria testing, and free essential medications.

💡 Youth Empowerment

We believe in sustainable change. We provide young people with vocational skills training, mentorship, and resources to help them build enterprises and become self-reliant leaders in society.
